## 5. Consistency review of borderline labels

After the first pass (4114 rows, 6 labeling batches running in parallel plus the pilot), all
borderline rows (1919: first-pass neutral, mixed, low-confidence, excluded, very short, or
containing a contrast word such as "but") were re-labeled **blind** by one reviewer per text length,
using the extra rules R1–R9. The review label is the final label.

- Agreement between first pass and blind review: **91.0%**
